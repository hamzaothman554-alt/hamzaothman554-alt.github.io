Case study · Al-Khair Real Estate

A real estate office that answers every lead, on every channel, in seconds

A complete system built for a real estate agency: a listing is entered once and published automatically to the website, Facebook and Telegram, while an AI assistant answers clients on WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook comments and a Telegram bot.

4 channels, one assistant A listing posted every hour, 9 am to 10 pm 6 recurring auto-posts 66 shipped releases

The problem

Messages arrive on WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger and Telegram, and the team can't keep up.
A client left waiting for hours goes to the next agency.
Every listing has to be written and posted by hand on each platform.

What it does

Instant comment replies

Anyone who comments "price" gets the price, details and link in a private message, and the comments lift the post's reach.

An assistant in the client's dialect

It understands "2-bedroom flat in this area", sends matching listings, and steps back when a staff member replies.

"Notify me"

When nothing matches, it saves the request and messages the client as soon as a fitting listing goes live.

Property owners

A form to submit a property, WhatsApp confirmation, and automatic updates when it's approved, reserved or sold.

A weekly content calendar

Latest listings, featured listings, "What your budget gets", prices by area, and tips, all posted automatically.
